Once the video is finished, I will send you a low-res (720p) heavily watermarked draft for preview and edits. The final clean high-quality video will be delivered only after the remaining 50% payment is received. All payments are non-refundable.
  • Revision Policy: You get 3 free revision rounds while working on the 720p watermarked draft. One revision round means a compiled, organized list of changes sent in a single message. Any edits requested after the final high-quality render or beyond the 3 rounds will cost +$15 per round.

If you have a specific vision or ideas, you must state them before I start. If you request a major concept change midway through because of a hidden idea, a +50% redesign fee will apply.
  • Technical Constraints: Base videos are rendered in 1080p at 24 fps (60 fps is not available). 4K resolution render is available as an extra add-on for +$30 to the total price.
  • Portfolio Rights & NDA:
    • Default: I retain the right to showcase the finished video or short snippets in my portfolio and social media after your official release.
    • Pre-release NDA: Keeping the project completely secret until your official upload date is **free** (just notify me in advance).
    • Permanent NDA (No Portfolio): If you want to permanently forbid me from ever posting the video or its parts in my portfolio, a +100% fee (double price) of the total order will apply.
